03 / TOKENS

A small, shared vocabulary.

TokenValue

At 720px and below, the gutter becomes 24px, section spacing 40px, and layout gap 24px. Fixed component spacing follows a 4px rhythm. Corner radius is shared at 12px; compact controls use 8px and circular arrows use 50%.

04 / QUALITY

Beauty should make things easier.

Legibility

Text / backgroundContrast

Ratios are calculated from the tokens above. Gold and subtle rules are decorative, not body text or focus indicators.

Interaction & reflow

  • At least 44px for standalone controls.
  • Site navigation collapses into a hamburger at 960px. Selection or Escape closes it.
  • Visible 2px keyboard focus, offset by 4px.
  • Up to three event cards per row, with a 220px minimum width and 24px gaps; columns adapt to available space.
  • Underlines are omitted; actions use color, placement, and arrows.
  • Hover lifts cards 4px in 180ms. Reduced motion removes translation and transitions.
  • Sponsors continue on hover. Keyboard focus and reduced motion reveal a static list.

These checks support accessibility; they are not a full audit.
